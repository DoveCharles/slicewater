Riff

Hold a key to play that chord. Turn Pad on to drone the chord until you turn it off. Turn Latch on and each key you tap sticks until you tap another (it changes chord) or tap the same root again (it lets go); turning Latch off releases it. Pump ducks the pad and sub on every kick that plays. While the beat runs, the sub plays the riff from the amber Bass lane (R root, 5 fifth, 8 octave, bar = hold) in whatever key the piano last played; pick Hold to sustain the root with the pad instead. Robot chords takes over the piano while the beat runs: it treats the chord you had as home, wanders through chords that belong in that key on the bar line you pick, and comes home every fourth change. Switch it off to go back to your chord. With Chord glide up, changing chord bends each ringing note to its nearest note in the new chord (try dragging across the keys); set it to off for clean cuts.

Lead

⇧ ShiftHold to play a melody

Hold Shift (or turn on Latch) and a mono sine lead improvises over the chord, using notes from a scale that fits the chord type and the key the piano last played. It locks to the beat when the beat is running and keeps its own clock when it isn't. Bars of melody often repeat with a new ending, so phrases come back around.

Sounds are generated with ZzFX by Frank Force (MIT). Each drum is two or three ZzFX calls layered; copy the code from any card to use the sound elsewhere. Kit or tempo changes re-render the break.
