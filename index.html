Riff

Hold a key to play that chord. Turn Pad on to drone the chord until you turn it off. Turn Latch on and each key you tap sticks until you tap another (it changes chord) or tap the same root again (it lets go); turning Latch off releases it. Pump ducks the pad and sub on every kick that plays. While the beat runs, the sub plays the riff from the amber Bass lane (R root, 5 fifth, 8 octave, bar = hold) in whatever key the piano last played; pick Hold to sustain the root with the pad instead. Robot chords takes over the piano while the beat runs: it treats the chord you had as home, wanders through chords that belong in that key in 8-bar phrases, each with its own rhythm of changes (like 2·1·1·4 or 1·1·2·2·2; the bracket shows where it is), and every phrase starts back home. Pace picks slower or busier rhythms. Turn on Parallel and it only moves the root: every chord keeps the type you have selected (change it while it runs if you like). Switch it off to go back to your chord. With Chord glide up, changing chord bends each ringing note to its nearest note in the new chord (try dragging across the keys); set it to off for clean cuts.

Lead

⇧ ShiftHold to play a melody

Hold Shift (or turn on Latch) and a mono sine lead improvises over the chord, using notes from a scale that fits the chord type and the key the piano last played. It locks to the beat when the beat is running and keeps its own clock when it isn't. Bars of melody often repeat with a new ending, so phrases come back around.
